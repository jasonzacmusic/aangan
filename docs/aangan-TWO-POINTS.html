The two doors — what goes where

G1 front door and the studio door · what to buy, what to connect, in what order

The plan

Two boards, two jobs. Board 6 sits just inside the G1 front door and handles everything there. Board 1 sits in the studio and handles both studio doors, the sound meter, the presence radar, the switch and the tally lights. Nothing crosses between the two boards — no cable runs from one end of the house to the other.

The studio is the connector between the G1 hall and the G2 hall, so it has a door on each side. That means a tally light at each one, or nobody approaching from G2 sees it.

Keep the chime you already have. Wire board 6 in parallel with the existing bell push, so the old chime still rings exactly as it does now — including when the Wi-Fi is down — and the board hears the same press and tells your phones. Do not remove anything.
The phone notification part is not built yet. The board can push straight to phones with no hub, through a free service. It is designed and it is my job, not a purchase. Until it exists, a doorbell press shows on the board's page but will not buzz anyone.

The two screens — do not buy yet

One at each door: the studio door showing recording status, the front door showing the delivery code. The app already has both built.

But the app still runs on simulated data and cannot read your boards. A screen showing invented information is worse than no screen. Buy these after that is connected.

For the wooden finish, I would use e-paper rather than a tablet — about ₹4,000 each. No backlight, no glare, and in a plain wooden frame it reads as an engraved sign rather than a gadget. A tablet would look wrong against that door.

The one thing to check before any screen is bought: is there a plug socket near each door? Near the studio door on the house side, and near the front door. If not, that is the only part of this whole project that needs an electrician.
