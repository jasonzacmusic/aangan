How noise alerts work

  1. The studio meter reports dB every few seconds.
  2. Settings holds the quiet line (default 45 dB, slider 35–70).
  3. If live dB is at or above that line, Pre-flight is Not Ready, Home goes red, and a banner says the studio is too loud.
  4. Turn on Studio too loud in Settings and tap Enable — the phone can banner while the app is open or installed to the home screen.
  5. A 3 a.m. ping with the phone locked is not built. That needs the ESP32 to push by itself.

Right now the empty studio is sitting at about 43–46 dB and the line is 45, so the verdict will flicker until we calibrate.

Calibrate with me

  1. The meter is already a real number. You do not need to prove the wire first.
  2. Message me quiet now while the room is take-quiet (doors shut, fan how you record).
  3. Then noisy now with the noisy thing (AC, fan, traffic).
  4. I set the slider just above the quiet sample. You do not type anything.

Leaf B of the studio door is ON / open. Close it (or fit that reed) or Rec will stay locked even after the threshold is right.
