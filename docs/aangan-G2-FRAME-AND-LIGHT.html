G2 lintel — teak frame and hall light

Print this for the wood shop. 20 Aug 2026. One 7″ glass in timber, one glowing bar above it. Same Aangan colours.

Not a gadget on the wall. A sign, and a tube of light.

The Waveshare 7″ is the picture. A teak frame hides the circuit. A 50 cm opal bar replaces the ring and the household bulb. Idle it is warm hall light. Rec / class / SOS it changes colour with the screen. Both listen to Board 1 — they cannot disagree.

1 · Carpenter — cut this, not a shop 7×5

Why not Amazon

A 7×5 photo frame opening is 178×127 mm. This panel is 193 × 111 mm. It will not go in. An 8×6 can be forced with a foam shim. A 20-minute teak rebate at SP Road / Commercial Street is the right object.

2 · How the ESP32 sits in the case

The 7″ board is the screen. It is already a computer.

  • It drops into the rebate from the back, glass facing the hall.
  • USB-C 5 V / 3 A brick in the old tube socket powers only the screen.
  • Flash it once on the bench with a data cable, then it lives on the wall brick.
  • Do not bury the board in a sealed box — it needs the back ply gap for air.
Do not hang the hall light off that same 3 A brick, and do not expect a spare GPIO on the 7″ for a long LED strip. The screen uses almost every pin for the picture. A 50 cm bar also draws more current than the panel can share. Colour still stays married: both listen to Board 1 on Wi-Fi.
The spare ESP32 already in the building (the one at 192.168.0.248) drives the bar. GPIO4 is already coded for colour. We change “12 LEDs in a ring” to “30 LEDs in a tube”. Same green / amber / red / SOS as the screen.

3 · The light — drop the ring

A 50 cm opal tube, not a puck and not a household bulb

Idle = warm white, so it is hall lighting. Rec = red. Class = blue. Too loud in the studio = amber. SOS = violet. The diffuser hides the dots. It reads as a modern tube above a picture, not a circuit.
