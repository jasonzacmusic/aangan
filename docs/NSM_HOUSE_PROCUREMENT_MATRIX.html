§ 03 — TWO THINGS TO CHECK

Where I'd pause before ordering

I'm flagging these honestly rather than letting you order into a gap. Both come from the matrix conflicting with your other files.

The door-sensor count

  • The matrix says buy 5 more MC-38 reeds — implying you already own some.
  • But your WhatsApp inventory shows zero door sensors.
  • And the blueprint needs around 12 to cover 3 double doors + bedrooms + balconies.

What to do: count the reeds you physically have. If it's zero, order 12, not 5 — they're about ₹50 each, so ~₹600 total. Cheapest fix in the whole build.

The gas sensor

  • The matrix specifies the MQ-2 hobby module (₹179) for kitchen gas.
  • An MQ-2 is fine as a data sensor for Home Assistant — but it is not a life-safety device.
  • Your kitchen has a real LPG cylinder (the photo confirms it).

What to do: keep the MQ-2 for logging if you like, but add a certified LPG alarm with its own siren as the primary. That's the one safety upgrade I'd insist on over the matrix.

The principle, once more: for anything that protects people — gas, fire, panic — a certified standalone device is primary and must work with no Pi and no Wi-Fi. The matrix is excellent on sourcing and price, but it's built around hobby sensors for these roles. Layer certified safety on top; don't let the hobby part be the only line of defence.
