1 · What Aangan is

Aangan (आंगन — the courtyard) is one system covering a family home and a working music studio in Bangalore. The organising idea is a single dial with six states — Available, Class, Meeting, Audio Rec, Video Rec, Emergency — and the whole house then behaves accordingly: door signs recolour, the doorbell mutes, AC and fan cut, the piano rig shows a tally, purifiers go quiet.

It replaced the name "Studio Command". The repo is jasonzacmusic/aangan, the local path is ~/Documents/Claude/aangan, and the app is live at studio-command.vercel.app (the Vercel project is still named studio-command, which is why the URL didn't change). Deploys are manual: vercel --prod --yes after pushing.

30 July — air, ventilation and the instruments

The idea behind all four: a purifier filters dust; it does nothing about CO₂. Only fresh air does. Measuring the difference is what makes this a system rather than a shelf of gadgets.
FeatureWhat it does
Recording-aware hush LIVEA purifier is a fan, and a fan lands on the take. The moment the dial goes to Audio or Video Rec, every purifier drops to silent; when the studio frees up they return to the exact mode they were in before — saved, not guessed. Verified in the browser: auto → silent → auto.
Pre-class purge LIVETwenty minutes before a calendar class the purifiers run flat out; the moment the class starts they go quiet. Students arrive to clean air and a silent room. Manual 10 and 20 minute purge buttons with a countdown, and it refuses to start during a take.
CO₂ ventilation nudge LIVEPast your threshold the panels and phones say "Crack the door — the room is going stale", and during a class it names the real cost: this is what makes students dull. A sealed, soundproofed studio is worse than an ordinary room because it's sealed by design.
Instrument climate guard LIVEA room outside 35–65% humidity for half an hour warns the family. A piano is wood and felt under tension; Bangalore's monsoon warps it and dry air cracks it, slowly and silently. Two cheap sensors protecting instruments worth lakhs.

Also in that pass: the Air card on Home (per-room CO₂, dust, odour index, humidity, plus per-purifier Off/Silent/Auto/Max), an air row in Pre-flight that is deliberately advice, not a gate — dust on camera is worth knowing but must never block recording, so studio_ready stays the original four checks — and Settings for the CO₂ threshold and the instrument humidity band.

A real bug found and fixed while testing: in the simulation, one open door anywhere was flushing CO₂ in every room at once and pinning it to the floor. It's now per-room with proportional (exponential) ventilation toward the 430 ppm outdoor baseline, which is how air actually behaves.

What I added on 31 July commit 7b2f2e6 · all 11 test suites green · all 3 plugin formats build

AdditionDetail
Stage CC mapChorale understood only two MIDI messages — trigger and expression — so a footswitch could not reach modes, voices or the doubler. It now accepts CC 20–26 for numVoices, harmonyMode2, doubler, dryLevel, harmonyLevel, stereoWidth, doublerVoices. CC 20–27 are undefined in the MIDI spec, so a pedal can own them without colliding with anything a keyboard sends.
Kept real-time safeThe audio thread only stores atomics; a 25 Hz message-thread pump does the parameter write, because setValueNotifyingHost is not RT-safe and your engine path is a zero-allocation gate. quality and sourceRange are deliberately excluded — they force a re-prepare, and a footswitch must never reallocate the engine mid-song.
A test that proves ittestStageFootswitchCCs asserts three things: the CC path allocates nothing on the callback, values land exactly on the right step, and the re-prepare parameters stay off the map.
Footswitch firmwaretools/pico-footswitch/code.py — CircuitPython on a Raspberry Pi Pico. Drag-and-drop install, no toolchain: copy one file to the CIRCUITPY drive and it is a MIDI device. No OS, no SD card, enumerates in under a second, cannot crash the way Linux can. Six switches: harmony, voices, mode, doubler, stage/studio, and a panic stomp that returns you to a safe audible state in one press. Expression pedal optional on CC 11.
Pi build in one commandscripts/build-pi.sh — installs JUCE's Linux dependencies, configures SCHED_FIFO permission, builds with -mcpu=cortex-a76, runs the RT-safety gate and the budget matrix, and prints the Mac reference numbers beside the Pi result.
Honest Linux measurementThe perf tool's non-Apple branch was a stub returning false, which would have measured scheduler noise rather than DSP cost. It now takes real SCHED_FIFO priority 80 on Linux.

The measurements — and the correction I owed you

My first answer was wrong. I benchmarked Chorale while the Vite dev server and browser from the Aangan work were still running, which inflated the result about 2.5×. I told you a Pi would need ~165% of a core and was therefore impossible. That was based on a contaminated measurement.

I also built and ran the entire thing without Apple's Accelerate framework to see what a Linux port would lose: 351.9 µs versus 347.4 µs at 128 samples. Within noise. So Accelerate is not a porting blocker either — the second multiplier in my estimate doesn't exist.

Revised verdict. A Pi 5's Cortex-A76 is roughly 4.5–6× slower per core than an M4 Pro core. Applied to 13.0%, Chorale lands at about 60–80% of one core with all six voices — tight but an engineering problem, not a physics problem, and cheaper with three or four voices or Live quality only. The Pi 5 has four cores and Chorale needs one. This is still an extrapolation; build-pi.sh replaces it with a real number in one afternoon.

What still blocks Chorale on the Pi

  • Gestures OPEN — GestureTrigger.mm is #if JUCE_MAC, built on AVFoundation and Apple's Vision framework. No Linux equivalent; this is a rewrite, not a port. I'd argue a camera is the wrong sensor for a stage anyway — coloured wash, backlight and haze all degrade pose detection, and the inference would compete for the cores the DSP needs. A time-of-flight or mmWave sensor reads hand height and arm spread for almost no CPU and doesn't care about lighting.
  • The audio board — see the next section. The DAC Pro cannot do this job.
  • The Linux UI — untested. For a stage box, headless with the Touch Display 2 showing the existing Stage Screen is probably the better answer.
  • A soak test — two hours at temperature before it goes near an audience.

4 · The hardware plan

The decision that affects the next order. The Raspberry Pi DAC Pro and its XLR daughter board are output-only. Pianoteq is happy on them, but Chorale is a harmonizer — it needs an analogue input and that board has none. Since both apps are meant to run on the music Pi, the board must have an ADC. Only one audio HAT can occupy the I2S bus, so this is a swap, not an addition.

The recommendation

HiFiBerry DAC2 ADC Pro — stereo line in and line out, 24-bit/192k, an input stage built for studio line level. One board serves Pianoteq (out) and Chorale (in and out). It is still a HAT: nothing external, nothing USB, consistent with wanting the audio baked into the Pi. The trade-off is real and yours to make: it doesn't mate with the official XLR daughter board, so balanced output comes back via two DI boxes.

Why there is no mic preamp on the list: on stage the console already has the preamp and the phantom power. Take a line-level send from the vocal channel into the Pi and return the harmonies to two spare channels. Line in, line out — exactly what the board is designed for.

Stage-worthiness — the parts that stop a Pi failing in front of an audience

PartWhy it is on the list
True-bypass looper pedalNon-negotiable. A mechanical relay that lifts the Pi out of the signal path with one stomp, so a crash or a reboot is a five-second recovery instead of silence in front of an audience. Software cannot be trusted to fail gracefully.
NVMe HAT + SSDBoot from SSD, not microSD. Card corruption on power loss is the most common way a Pi dies in the field, and the card is the part that dies.
UPS HATRides out a brownout or a pulled cable long enough to shut down cleanly.
Pico ×2, footswitches ×6, 1590BB enclosureThe pedal. Metal enclosure, not plastic — it gets stood on. Two Picos so there's a spare in the case.

5 · Traps and findings worth keeping

FindingWhy it matters
Never benchmark with a dev server runningCost me a wrong answer on the biggest architectural question in this project. 2.5× inflation.
timeout does not exist on macOStimeout N cmake --build … silently does nothing, and you then test a stale binary and believe it passed. Always check the binary's timestamp after a build.
Apple Accelerate makes no difference hereMeasured both ways. Don't treat it as a porting blocker.
CC 64 is the sustain pedalChorale's trigger defaults to CC 64. A keyboard sharing the MIDI port will toggle your harmony with its sustain pedal, mid-song. Move it to CC 27 before any gig and match the firmware.
Stepped MIDI values must be exactA stepped parameter needs round(i/(n-1)×127) or you land on 5 voices when you asked for 6. The firmware computes these.
Never smart-plug a purifierMost don't resume their previous speed when power returns — some need a physical button press. The house would silently stop purifying and nobody would notice. An exhaust fan is fine; it has no state to lose.
A purifier cannot fix CO₂It filters particles. Only fresh air removes CO₂ — which is why that feature is a ventilation nudge, not a purifier rule.
Double doors: sensor at the meeting stilesSwitch on one leaf, magnet on the other, in the middle. Either leaf opening trips it, so one sensor covers the whole door.
Certified alarms stay primaryAnything protecting people — gas, fire, panic — must work with no Pi and no Wi-Fi. The hobby sensors are the smart overlay, never the only line of defence. The panic loop is wired by a low-voltage/fire-alarm technician, and the ESP32s only observe volt-free contacts.
Dry never goes through the box on stageChorale's ON STAGE scene already sets dryLevel = 0: the vocal goes direct to front-of-house at zero latency and Chorale contributes harmonies only. This side-steps the 22–55 ms FFT window entirely for the lead.
