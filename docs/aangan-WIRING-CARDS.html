Aangan — wiring cards

Six boards, 36 sensors · generated from the firmware actually flashed · 13 August 2026

How to use this. One card per board. Find the number on the tape, wire each row, tick it. Every pin number is printed in tiny white text along the edge of the board — look for the bare number, so 25 may read D25, G25 or IO25.

The one rule: only ever connect a sensor between a numbered pin and a GND pin. Never touch 3V3, 5V or VIN to ground — that is a short circuit and kills the board.

Each board runs from any micro-USB phone charger. Test on the bench before anything goes on a wall.

This is the only board needing a soldering iron. Each of the four gas sensors needs two resistors between it and its pin: sensor output to a 10k, then the pin, then a 20k down to ground. Without them the sensor can push 5V into a 3.3V pin and damage the board. The four heaters also need their own 5V supply, not the ESP32 — they draw about 600mA together.

Do not aim the flame sensor at the hob, or it will alarm every time you cook.
Power this board tonight, even bare. The gas sensors need 24-48 hours of continuous power before their readings settle.
